# AI Business Prompt Engineering

A practical prompt-engineering project demonstrating how simple AI prompts can be transformed into **structured, reliable, and business-ready prompts**.

This repository contains examples covering **business research, marketing, finance, HR, operations, customer analysis, and AI-assisted decision-making**.

The project focuses not only on getting an answer from AI, but on improving the quality of the prompt by adding **context, role, constraints, required information, output format, verification, and human review**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to demonstrate the progression of AI prompting:

**Basic Prompt → Refined Prompt → Business-Ready Prompt**

A strong business prompt connects:

**Business Problem → Required Information → AI Task → Output → Human Review → Business Decision**

AI is treated as a tool that provides useful input, while important business decisions remain the responsibility of the human decision-maker.

## 🧠 Key Prompt-Engineering Concepts

This project demonstrates how prompts become more useful when they specify:

### Role

Tell the AI what role it should perform.

Example:

> Act as a marketing manager.

### Context

Provide the business situation and background.

Example:

> A café is located near a university and wants to increase weekday visits.

### Task

Clearly explain what the AI needs to do.

Example:

> Create a 7-day promotional campaign.

### Constraints

Define limitations that the AI must follow.

Example:

> Keep the total budget below ₹20,000.

### Output Format

Tell AI exactly how the answer should be presented.

Example:

> Present the answer in a table with Day, Activity, Channel, Cost and Expected Benefit.

### Verification

Require important claims and assumptions to be checked.

Example:

> Clearly identify information that requires verification.

## 🔍 Research & Evidence Approach

A major part of the project focuses on preventing unsupported AI-generated claims.

The research prompts encourage users to:

1. Define a focused research question
2. Use AI to plan research
3. Generate search keywords
4. Find original sources
5. Read the sources
6. Verify important claims
7. Build an evidence table
8. Compare sources
9. Develop business insights
10. Write with citations
11. Conduct final human review

The documents specifically emphasize that statistics and sources should be **verified rather than invented**.

## 📝 Example: Prompt Evolution

### Basic

> Suggest a marketing campaign.

### Refined

> Suggest five low-cost promotional ideas for a café near a university targeting students aged 18–24.

### Business-Ready

> Act as a marketing manager for a café located near a university. The café wants to increase weekday visits from college students aged 18–24. Create a 7-day promotional campaign with a budget of ₹20,000. Present the answer in a structured table and clearly identify assumptions.

The final version gives the AI significantly more context and makes the output easier to evaluate and use.

## 🛡️ Responsible AI Principles

This project follows several important principles:

* Do not invent statistics.
* Do not invent sources.
* Verify important claims against original sources.
* Clearly distinguish facts from assumptions.
* Separate evidence from interpretation.
* Identify missing information.
* Use multiple reliable sources when appropriate.
* Keep human review in the decision-making process.

For example, the research prompts recommend checking the publication date, data year, geography, market definition, publisher, and supporting evidence before accepting an AI-generated market figure.
